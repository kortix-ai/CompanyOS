# Offer outline (when sales.json names no template)

A plain, printable HTML page: A4 or Letter, one column, the company's name
and website in the header, system fonts, black text on white, one accent
color from the company's website. At most 3 pages.

1. **Title.** `<Package> for <customer>` and the date.
2. **Your goal.** Two to four sentences: what the customer wants to achieve,
   in the words of the deal notes. Cite nothing in the page; keep the sources
   in the deal note.
3. **What we propose.** The scope as a short list: what is included, what is
   not.
4. **The package.** A table: package name, what it includes, the price and the
   billing period exactly as `offers.packages` states them, in
   `offers.currency`.
5. **How we start.** Three to five steps with owners: "you" and "us".
6. **Validity and next step.** "This offer is valid until <date>." One next
   step: a call, a reply, a signature on a separate order form.
7. **Contact.** `company.sender` name and title.

No signature block, no "Accept" button, no terms and conditions: a person
attaches the binding documents.
