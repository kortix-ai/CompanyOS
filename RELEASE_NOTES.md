A calm session chat, and replies that never vanish

### Improved

- The session chat holds still while your agents work. No blank space appears or disappears under your message, and sending a message no longer moves the conversation.
- "Worked for" stays the way you leave it. It never opens or closes by itself, and the agent's words to you always stay in view; only its steps fold.
- Reports from sub-agents and teammates fold into one "Worked for" row under the message that started the work, with a live count of the agents that reported. A report that asks you a question joins that row too.
- Updates from other agents no longer appear in the waiting list above the composer, and the cards above the composer ease in and out instead of snapping.
- My Kortix makes and changes schedules and automations only through its scheduling tool, and an automation goes live as soon as its test run's session finished.

### Fixed

- Assistant replies no longer disappear until a hard refresh. A session-log read that stalled after its first bytes blocked every later re-read; reads now give up after 20 seconds and the session catches up by itself.
- A message you sent while the agent worked could stay below newer replies; it now moves to its real place.
- The workspace trigger list no longer fails when one automation has no schedule.
- Promoting a hidden helper agent to a primary agent now shows it in the agent picker, and "stop the report" pauses a schedule instead of deleting it.
