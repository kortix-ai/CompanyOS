# Platforms

Platform limits change. A person who sees the composer refuse a draft corrects
this table in a commit.

| Platform | Limit | House rule |
|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters per post (standard account); a URL counts as 23 | One idea, one link, no hashtags, no emoji unless `voice.md` allows them. A thread only for a step-by-step: at most 5 posts, each standing alone. |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters per post | 80 to 200 words. The first line states the outcome in plain words (it shows before "see more"). One link, at the end. |
| Other (`channels.social.platforms`) | read the platform's own help page and record the limit in this table | the same: outcome first, one proof link |

## Shape of a good post

- First line: what the reader can now do. Not "We shipped …".
- Second part: how, in one or two concrete steps or one number from proof.
- Last: the proof link.
