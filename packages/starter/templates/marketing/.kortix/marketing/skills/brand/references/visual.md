## Reject list

Reject an image or a video frame that shows any of these:

- a generated logo, or a logo in a color the table does not list;
- generated product UI;
- unreadable, misspelled or cut-off text;
- a real person's face, name, email or account id that is not synthetic;
- a large area in a color the table does not list;
- stock clichés: glowing brains, robots shaking hands, abstract blue networks.
