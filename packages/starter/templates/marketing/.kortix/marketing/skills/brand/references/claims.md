## Never claim

- A customer, logo, quote or case study that is not on the live site.
- A number (users, stars, speed, savings) without a proof row in this table.
- "First", "only" or "best" because a search found nothing else.
- A partnership or endorsement the partner did not publish.
- A price or a discount: link the pricing page instead.
